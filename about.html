A mission to be the go-to spot for quality, affordable food near GBU. A true Indian Adda.

Our Story

Located in Gautam Buddha University, Kasna Village, The Food Adda (TFA) is more than just a cafeteria. It is a conceptual space designed by students, for students.

We understand that university life is demanding. That’s why we’ve created a spot where you can find the best Parathas and Burgers at rates that respect a student budget. Our doors at Shop Number 7 are open daily from 10:30 AM to 11:00 PM, ensuring you always have a place to recharge.
